Warranty & Returns Policy
Returns & Exchanges
  • Unopened items: returnable within [7] days for a refund or exchange, with the seal intact.
  • Once opened: a change-of-mind return no longer applies.
  • Faulty items: returnable or replaceable within [7] days once the fault is confirmed.
What is covered
  • Manufacturing or material defects found under normal use within the return window.
  • Any longer manufacturer warranty stated on the product itself.
What is not covered
  • Physical damage: drops, cracks, bent or broken connectors.
  • Liquid, heat, fire or electrical damage, including power surges.
  • Damage from incorrect installation or incompatible parts.
  • Unauthorised modification, or a removed or defaced serial number.
  • Continued use after a fault appeared.
Non-returnable
  • Digital products and licence keys.
  • Opened hygiene items.
  • Clearance and as-is items.
  • Used consumables.
Refunds
  • Refunds are issued by the method of payment.
  • An item bought at a discount is refunded at the price paid.
To make a claim
  • Proof of purchase: this receipt, or the phone number or email it was issued to.
  • The item returned within the stated window.
